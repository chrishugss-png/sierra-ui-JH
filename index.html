The Approach

Three steps to Generative UI.

A controlled path from proven voice capability to adaptive interfaces. Each step earns the next.

STEP 01

Sierra text agentic agents

Migrate proven voice agents into messaging through digital entry points. Establish that customers can complete high-value journeys through text before adding visual complexity.

Foundation
STEP 02

Deterministic dynamic UI

Introduce approved, predefined interface components at controlled points in the journey. Prove the incremental value of visual and interactive elements against the text baseline.

Controlled
STEP 03

Non-deterministic Generative UI

Enable dynamically composed interfaces through Sierra and GECX - only when the evidence, governance and controls from steps one and two support it.

Adaptive

Delivery Approach

An internal delivery model that reduces TCS dependency.

The primary delivery risk is competition for engineering resources within TCS and the delivery of the voice agentic agents. To reduce this dependency, the CCAI team has developed an internal delivery model.

CCAI Responsibilities
Internal delivery model
  • Discovery
  • Influence and support end-to-end solution design
  • User interface prototyping and front-end development
TCS Responsibilities
Backend and governance
  • Solution design
  • Standard backend API integration
  • Governance sign-off
Risks & Dependencies

Two dependencies to actively manage.

Both risks sit outside the CCAI team's direct control. The delivery model above is designed to reduce exposure to the first; the second requires close coordination with the DPPA team.

Risk 01 - Resource

Competition for TCS engineering resource

TCS engineering capacity is shared across multiple concurrent programmes. Contention could slow backend integration and governance sign-off for the Sierra AI build.

Mitigation The CCAI internal delivery model reduces exposure by owning discovery, front-end prototyping and design influence in-house - leaving TCS to focus only on standard backend API integration and governance.
Risk 02 - Dependency

Delivery of the voice agentic agents by the DPPA team

The programme depends on the voice agents being built by the DPPA team. Sierra's messaging agents extend those voice capabilities, so any slippage in voice delivery will directly impact the timeline above.

Mitigation Close coordination with the DPPA team, aligned delivery milestones, and early visibility of voice agent readiness feeding into the sequencing shown in the delivery timeline.
